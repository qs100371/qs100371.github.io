天气来源

https://api.openweathermap.org/data/2.5/weather?q=beijing&appid=xxxxxxxx&units=metric&lang=zh_cn

https://api.openweathermap.org/data/2.5/weather?lat=39.91&lon=116.40&appid=xxxxxxxx&units=metric&lang=zh_cn
